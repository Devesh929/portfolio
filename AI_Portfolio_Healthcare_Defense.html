Profile

From prediction
to high-stakes reasoning.

I started with classical ML pipelines. Today, I engineer private AI systems that combine fine-tuned language models, multimodal data, telemetry, deterministic controls and human-in-the-loop validation.

“The model is only one layer. The real system is context, reasoning, validation and action.”
Devesh Chaudhary · AI Engineer

Nearly six years across classical ML, deep learning and applied AI shaped an engineering-first approach: understand the failure mode, build a traceable intelligence layer, deploy it where the data lives, and measure whether it improves a real decision.

For the last three years, the focus has moved deeper into LLM fine-tuning, local inference, multimodal reasoning and telemetry intelligence — spanning healthcare decision support, defense edge systems, manufacturing, e-mobility and other environments where privacy, latency, evidence and reliability matter as much as model capability.

Evidence over fluencyAnswers are tied to retrieved context, signals, rules and confidence.
Private by architectureLocal, edge and air-gapped inference paths when data cannot leave the environment.
Multimodal by defaultReason across documents, images, tables and high-frequency telemetry together.
Human control stays visibleAbstention, validation and operator review are designed into critical workflows.
